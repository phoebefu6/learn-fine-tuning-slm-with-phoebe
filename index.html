Learn Fine-tuning and Small Language Models with Phoebe

Where the AI shelf stops: when a smaller tuned model beats the big general one, and how to prove it with evals. The six sessions 🧭 · from "should we?" to a signed decision memo

The running case is Larkline, a constructed mid-size insurer. Its claims desk routes about 900,000 messages a month to six queues, one of them urgent, with a prompt on a big general API model. It is slow at peak and costly at volume. Priya, its ML engineer, has to answer one question: should Larkline fine-tune a small model instead? Larkline and everyone in it are invented for teaching, and every page says so.
